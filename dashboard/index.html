How the implied value is worked out

implied = round( TVL × comp(category) × growth × maturity × chains )

  • comp(category) — the median market-cap / TVL ratio of the snapshot’s live-token protocols in that category, each ratio capped at 10, shrunk toward the global median with weight n/(n+10); the global median when a category has fewer than two live comps. Clamped to [0.05, 1.0], rounded to 4 decimals.
  • growth = 1 + clamp(change_7d, −60, 120) / 100 × 0.5 — a protocol growing fast is worth more than one shrinking.
  • maturity = 0.6 + min(age_days, 1095) / 1095 × 0.4 — age is the days since DefiLlama first listed it; unlisted counts as 365 days.
  • chains = 1 + 0.02 × min(max(chains − 1, 0), 5) — a small premium for being live on more chains.
